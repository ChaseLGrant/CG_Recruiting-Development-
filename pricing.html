Our Commitment to You

We don't guarantee scholarships—no one can. What we guarantee is a process: consistent training, strategic recruiting guidance, and real support from people who've been through it. We're invested in your progress, and we'll work alongside you every step of the way. If you follow the plan and put in the work, you'll be in the best position possible to reach your goals.

All memberships are month-to-month. Cancel anytime with no penalty. Annual prepay plans can be refunded on a prorated basis.

Questions About Pricing?

We understand that life happens. You can pause your membership for up to 30 days per year. Just let us know in advance, and we'll hold your spot without charging you during the pause period.
