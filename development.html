Player Development

Build real skills through personalized training. Private lessons, video analysis, metric tracking, and a plan that actually moves you forward.

Development Without a Plan Is Just Practice

Most athletes practice. Few actually develop. The difference? A plan. We don't just run you through drills—we assess where you are, identify what needs work, build a personalized program, and track your progress over time.

Every lesson builds on the last. Every rep has a purpose. And every metric gets tracked so you can see real improvement—not just feel like you're working hard.

[Training Session Image]

What We Train

Position-specific, skill-focused development tailored to your goals.

Hitting

Mechanics, bat speed, approach, pitch recognition. We use video analysis and exit velocity tracking to measure real improvement.

Pitching

Velocity, command, movement, arm health. We focus on efficient mechanics and building a pitch arsenal that gets outs.

Fielding

Footwork, glove work, arm strength, reads. Position-specific training for infielders, outfielders, and catchers.

Speed & Agility

First-step quickness, base running, 60-yard dash improvement. Speed is a skill—and it can be trained.

Video Analysis

Submit videos between sessions for detailed feedback. We break down your swing or delivery frame by frame.

Strength Coordination

We coordinate with your strength and conditioning to make sure gym work translates to on-field performance.

How Lessons Work

Structured, focused, and designed to build on each other.

1

Initial Assessment

We evaluate your current skills, identify strengths and weaknesses, and establish baseline metrics.

2

Build Your Plan

Based on your assessment and goals, we create a personalized development plan with clear priorities.

3

Weekly Sessions

One-on-one lessons focused on your plan. Each session builds on the last with specific drills and progressions.

4

Track & Adjust

We monitor your progress, update your metrics, and adjust the plan as you improve.
