Recruiting Advising

The recruiting process is confusing, competitive, and full of misinformation. We cut through the noise with a strategic, personalized approach—helping you identify the right schools, craft compelling outreach, and manage the timeline so nothing falls through the cracks.

School targeting based on your profile and goals
Coach outreach strategy and messaging
Timeline management (sophomore through senior)
Camp and showcase recommendations

Player Development

Skill development is the foundation of everything. Our private lessons are tailored to your position, skill level, and goals—whether you're working on hitting mechanics, pitching velocity, fielding fundamentals, or all three. Every session builds on the last.

One-on-one private lessons
Personalized development plan
Hitting, pitching, and fielding focus
Video analysis and feedback

Metrics & Strength Coordination

Numbers matter in recruiting. We track the metrics that college coaches care about—and help you improve them. From velocity and exit speed to sprint times and pop time, we measure what matters and build a plan to move the needle.

Baseline and ongoing metric assessments
Fastball velocity, exit velo, 60-yard dash
Coordination with strength and conditioning
Progress tracking dashboard
[Social Media / NIL Image]

NIL / Social Branding

Your personal brand starts before you get to campus. NIL (Name, Image, and Likeness) opportunities are growing, and coaches notice athletes with strong social presence. We help you build a professional, authentic brand that opens doors.

Social media profile optimization
Content strategy and ideas
Highlight video guidance
NIL education and preparation
