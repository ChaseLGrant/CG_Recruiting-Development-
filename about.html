About CG Recruiting & Development

Built by coaches who've been through the process. Designed for athletes who want more than just lessons.

[Founder/Team Photo]

Our Story

CG Recruiting & Development was built out of frustration—with the recruiting process, with the training industry, and with how many talented athletes fall through the cracks because they don't have the right guidance.

We've seen it firsthand: athletes with real potential who don't know how to market themselves. Families overwhelmed by conflicting advice. Players stuck in lesson cycles that don't lead anywhere.

So we built something different. A system that combines real development with real recruiting strategy. Where every lesson has a purpose. Where parents are informed, not sidelined. Where the goal isn't just to train—it's to help athletes reach their potential and find the right opportunity.

Our Mission

To give every serious athlete—regardless of background—access to the same quality training, recruiting strategy, and personal guidance that used to be reserved for the elite.

What We Believe

Process Over Promises

We don't guarantee scholarships. We guarantee a process—one that maximizes your opportunity through consistent work.

Clarity Over Hype

No inflated promises. No buzzwords. Just honest advice and a clear plan based on where you actually are.

Families Included

Parents aren't an afterthought. You're part of the process—informed, included, and confident in what we're building together.

Measure What Matters

We track progress with real data—not feelings. Numbers don't lie, and they help us adjust the plan as you grow.
