# High-Converting Baseball Recruiting & Development Website Prompt

Use this comprehensive prompt to build a professional, high-converting website for your personal baseball recruiting and development business.

---

## Website Overview

**Business Type:** Personal Baseball Recruiting & Player Development Services

**Primary Goals:**
1. Generate leads from parents and athletes seeking recruiting guidance
2. Book private lessons and training sessions
3. Establish authority and trust in the baseball community
4. Convert visitors into paying clients

---

## Target Audience

### Primary Audiences:
1. **High School Athletes (Ages 14-18)** - Players looking to play at the next level (college/pro)
2. **Parents of Athletes** - Decision-makers who pay for services
3. **Youth Players (Ages 10-14)** - Developing players building foundational skills
4. **College Athletes** - Players seeking pro exposure or transfer opportunities

### Audience Pain Points to Address:
- Confusion about the recruiting process and timeline
- Uncertainty about what college coaches are looking for
- Lack of exposure to the right programs/scouts
- Need for personalized skill development
- Frustration with generic training programs
- Fear of missing recruiting opportunities

---

## Website Structure & Pages

### 1. Homepage (Hero Section)

**Headline Formula:** [Result] + [Timeframe] + [Without Common Objection]

**Example Headlines:**
- "Get Recruited to Play College Baseball — Even If You've Been Overlooked"
- "Turn Your Baseball Dreams Into Scholarship Offers"
- "Expert Recruiting Guidance & Elite Training for Serious Players"

**Hero Section Must Include:**
- Compelling headline addressing the main desire
- Subheadline explaining HOW you help
- Clear call-to-action button ("Schedule Free Consultation" / "Start Your Journey")
- Background image/video of training or game action
- Trust indicators (years of experience, players placed, success stats)

**Social Proof Bar:**
- Number of players placed in college programs
- Colleges/universities your players have committed to
- Years of experience
- Any playing/coaching credentials

---

### 2. Services Page

**Service Categories to Offer:**

#### A. Recruiting Services
- **Recruiting Assessment** - Evaluate player's college readiness
- **Recruiting Profile Creation** - Build professional player profiles
- **College Outreach Campaigns** - Direct contact with coaches
- **Video Production & Editing** - Highlight reels that get noticed
- **Showcase/Camp Guidance** - Which events to attend
- **Commitment Guidance** - Navigate offers and decisions

#### B. Player Development / Private Lessons
- **One-on-One Training** - Hitting, pitching, fielding
- **Position-Specific Training** - Specialized skill work
- **Strength & Conditioning Programs**
- **Mental Performance Coaching**
- **Small Group Training** - 2-4 players

#### C. Package Options (Tiered Pricing)
```
TIER 1: "PROSPECT" - Entry-level recruiting guidance
TIER 2: "COMPETITOR" - Full recruiting support + limited training
TIER 3: "ELITE" - All-inclusive recruiting + unlimited training
```

**Each Service Should Include:**
- Clear description of what's included
- Who it's best for
- Expected outcomes/results
- Pricing or "Contact for Pricing"
- CTA button to book/inquire

---

### 3. About Page

**Elements to Include:**

1. **Your Story**
   - Playing background (high school, college, pro if applicable)
   - Coaching/recruiting experience
   - Why you started this business
   - Your mission and philosophy

2. **Credentials & Qualifications**
   - Playing career highlights
   - Coaching certifications
   - Schools coached at
   - Professional affiliations

3. **Your Approach**
   - What makes your training different
   - Your recruiting philosophy
   - How you evaluate and develop players

4. **Personal Photo**
   - Professional headshot
   - Action photos coaching/playing

---

### 4. Success Stories / Results Page

**This is your most important conversion page.**

**Include:**
- Player testimonials with photos
- College commitment announcements
- Before/after skill improvements
- Video testimonials (most powerful)
- List of colleges where players have committed
- Stats and metrics (exit velo improvements, ERA drops, etc.)

**Testimonial Format:**
```
"[Specific result achieved]"
- Player Name, Position, High School → College Committed To
[Photo of player]
```

---

### 5. Resources / Blog Page

**Content Ideas for SEO & Authority:**
- "Complete Guide to the College Baseball Recruiting Timeline"
- "What College Coaches Actually Look For in Recruits"
- "How to Create a Baseball Recruiting Video That Gets Watched"
- "D1 vs D2 vs D3 vs NAIA vs JUCO: Which Is Right for You?"
- "Top Showcases and Camps Worth Attending in [Your Region]"
- "How to Email College Coaches (With Templates)"
- "The Truth About Baseball Scholarships"
- "Training Tips for [Specific Position]"

---

### 6. Contact / Book a Consultation Page

**Form Fields:**
- Parent/Guardian Name
- Player Name
- Player Age / Graduation Year
- Position(s)
- Current School
- Phone Number
- Email
- "What are your baseball goals?" (text area)
- How did you hear about us?

**Page Should Include:**
- Multiple contact methods (phone, email, form)
- Response time expectation
- Location/service area
- Embedded calendar for scheduling (Calendly, etc.)

---

## Conversion Optimization Elements

### Must-Have Throughout Site:

1. **Sticky Header CTA** - "Free Consultation" button always visible

2. **Exit-Intent Popup** - Offer free resource:
   - "Download: The Complete College Baseball Recruiting Checklist"
   - "Free Guide: 10 Mistakes That Kill Your Recruiting Chances"

3. **Lead Magnets:**
   - Recruiting timeline PDF
   - College coach email templates
   - Training program samples
   - Video requirements checklist

4. **Urgency Elements:**
   - "Recruiting windows are short — start early"
   - Limited spots for training
   - Seasonal enrollment periods

5. **Trust Signals:**
   - College logos where players have committed
   - Professional certifications
   - Years in business
   - Total players helped

6. **FAQ Section** addressing:
   - Cost/pricing questions
   - What age to start recruiting process
   - How long until results
   - What's included in packages
   - Refund/satisfaction guarantees

---

## Design Specifications

### Color Palette Suggestions:
- **Primary:** Navy Blue or Deep Red (baseball traditional)
- **Secondary:** White, Gray
- **Accent:** Gold/Yellow (for CTAs and highlights)

### Typography:
- Headlines: Bold, athletic-style fonts
- Body: Clean, readable sans-serif

### Imagery:
- High-quality action photos
- Training session photos
- Player success photos (commitments)
- Stadium/field backgrounds
- Avoid generic stock photos

### Mobile Optimization:
- 60%+ of traffic will be mobile
- Click-to-call phone numbers
- Easy form completion
- Fast loading images

---

## Technical Requirements

1. **Speed:** Page load under 3 seconds
2. **SSL Certificate:** HTTPS for security/trust
3. **SEO Basics:**
   - Meta titles and descriptions
   - Image alt tags
   - Local SEO (Google Business Profile)
   - Schema markup for local business

4. **Analytics:**
   - Google Analytics 4
   - Conversion tracking on form submissions
   - Heatmaps (Hotjar/Microsoft Clarity)

5. **Integrations:**
   - Email marketing (Mailchimp, ConvertKit)
   - Scheduling tool (Calendly, Acuity)
   - CRM for lead management
   - Payment processing (Stripe, Square)
